01LMS website QA & UI audit

Does it work, read clearly and behave the same everywhere?

The objective: confirm that CloudSwyft's learning-management website works, reads clearly and behaves consistently, and document it so the team can act on what I find.

  1. ScopeMap pages and features to test
  2. Write casesFront-end and back-end test cases
  3. ExecuteRun each case and record pass/fail
  4. Log findingsNote issues and UI inconsistencies
  5. DocumentOutline technical documentation

How the QA Audit Form is organized

I built and maintain the QA Audit Form in Google Sheets. Each case is written so anyone can reproduce it, and ends with a status, a severity and a recommendation.

0633 Rewards System proposal

Beyond Transactions.

A 35-slide proposal I prepared for CloudSwyft Marketing: Integrating the 33 Rewards System Across CloudSwyft Programs. It adapts the 33 rewards to CloudSwyft's learning platform, virtual labs and client relationships, and lays out how the company could test them before committing.

Six reward categories

All 33 rewards, grouped by what they recognise: buying, access, participation, referrals, surprise and loyalty.

#1–8 Purchase-Based#9–15 Exclusive Access#16–20 Engagement#21–24 Referral & People#25–28 Fun Surprises#29–33 Retention

Implementation roadmap

Five phases, each with a lead team, a deliverable and a gate to pass before moving on. It starts with a small pilot and only assumes LMS or CRM tracking once that's confirmed.

  1. Validate & MapGate: management approves design and budget
  2. PilotGate: positive signal at acceptable cost
  3. TrackGate: records reliable and auditable
  4. Launch & CommunicateGate: rollout to eligible segments
  5. Evaluate & ExpandGate: continuous cycle
